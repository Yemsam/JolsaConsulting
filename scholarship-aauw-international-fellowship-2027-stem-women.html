AAUW International Fellowship 2027 (USA, STEM Master's & PhD for Women)

The AAUW International Fellowship supports women pursuing full-time STEM graduate degrees at accredited U.S. institutions, with awards of $20,000 (Master's) or $25,000 (PhD) for the 2027–2028 academic year.

What is the AAUW International Fellowship 2027?

The AAUW (American Association of University Women) International Fellowship supports women from around the world who are pursuing full-time graduate study or research in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) disciplines at accredited U.S. institutions. Since 1917, the program has awarded fellowships to over 3,600 women from more than 150 countries.

The fellowship is designed for women who are non-U.S. citizens or permanent residents, with a track record of academic excellence and a demonstrated commitment to empowering other women and girls. Fellows are expected to return to their home countries to apply their expertise in service of their communities.

What does it cover?

  • Master's degree candidates: $20,000 stipend for the 2027–2028 academic year
  • Doctoral degree candidates: $25,000 stipend for the 2027–2028 academic year
  • Funds can be applied toward tuition, university fees, living expenses, required books, supplies, and dependent care
  • Disbursed in two equal installments at the start and midpoint of the fellowship term
  • Note: Tax withholding of 14% or 30% may apply depending on tax treaties and status

Who is eligible?

  • Must identify as a woman
  • Must be a citizen of a country other than the United States and not a U.S. permanent resident
  • Must be admitted to or enrolled in a first Master's degree or academic/professional doctoral degree in a STEM discipline at an accredited U.S. institution
  • Must hold at least one academic degree (earned in the U.S. or abroad) equivalent to a U.S. bachelor's degree
  • Must have a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.5 on a 4.0 scale for the highest degree earned
  • Must begin the program on or before September 15, 2027, and complete it on or after April 30, 2028
  • Must demonstrate a commitment to advancing women and girls through civic, community, or professional work
  • Proficiency in English required (TOEFL iBT minimum 79 or IELTS minimum 6.5)

Application timeline

  • August 17, 2026: Application portal opens at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time
  • September 17, 2026: Application deadline at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time
  • Spring 2027: Fellowship recipients notified
  • July 1, 2027 – June 30, 2028: Fellowship year

How to apply

  1. Prepare your documents early. You will need: proof of admission or enrollment at a U.S. institution for a STEM program; official transcripts; proof of English language proficiency (TOEFL or IELTS scores unless exempt); a detailed CV using the AAUW template (maximum 3 pages); and two letters of recommendation.
  2. Create an account on the AAUW Fellowships & Grants Application Portal at the official AAUW website.
  3. Complete the online application form, including your personal statement explaining your academic goals, commitment to women and girls, and how you will use your education to make an impact in your home country or community.
  4. Request recommendation letters early. Recommenders can submit letters before you submit your application, and all letters must be received by the September 17 deadline.
  5. Submit your application before 5:00 p.m. ET on September 17, 2026. Late applications will not be accepted under any circumstances.

Application tips

  • Start early—AAUW emphasizes that competitive applications require careful preparation
  • Use the official AAUW CV template and follow all formatting instructions exactly; applications that don't comply may be disqualified
  • In your personal statement, be specific about how your STEM expertise will empower women and girls in your community or home country
  • Choose recommenders who can speak to both your academic ability and your leadership and commitment to advancing women's equity
  • If you are in your final year of undergraduate study, you may apply as long as you will hold a bachelor's degree before the fellowship year begins
  • Only apply for one degree type (Master's or Doctoral) in one specific STEM field

FAQs

Can I apply if I'm already enrolled in a U.S. program?
Yes, as long as you meet all other eligibility requirements and will be enrolled full-time during the 2027–2028 fellowship year.

Are postdoctoral studies supported?
The International Fellowship supports Master's and Doctoral study only. AAUW offers separate International Postdoctoral Research Fellowships for postdoctoral candidates.

Can the fellowship be renewed for a second year?
A limited number of Master's degree fellowships (up to five) may be renewed for a second year on a competitive basis, but you must maintain a minimum GPA of 3.5 and submit renewal documentation by May 30, 2027.

What STEM fields are eligible?
Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics, and Medicine. Check the official AAUW website for the complete list of eligible disciplines.

Always verify current dates, award amounts, and eligibility directly on the official AAUW International Fellowships website: AAUW International Fellowships.
