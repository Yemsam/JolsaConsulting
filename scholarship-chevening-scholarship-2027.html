What is the Chevening Scholarship?

Chevening is the UK government's international scholarships programme, funded by the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) and partner organisations. It funds a fully-funded, one-year taught master's degree at any UK university for future leaders, influencers, and decision-makers from eligible countries, including Nigeria.

What does it cover?

  • Full tuition fees for a one-year taught master's degree
  • A monthly stipend of £1,378–£1,690 (higher in London) to cover living costs
  • Return economy airfare to and from the UK
  • An arrival allowance, a departure allowance, and a travel allowance to attend a Chevening event
  • The cost of one UK visa application

Who is eligible?

  • Be a citizen of a Chevening-eligible country (Nigeria is eligible) and intend to return there for at least two years after your award ends
  • Hold an undergraduate degree that will enable you to gain entry onto a postgraduate programme at a UK university
  • Have at least two years of work experience (approximately 2,800 hours), which can be full-time, part-time, voluntary, or paid
  • Apply to three different eligible UK master's courses and have received an unconditional offer from one by the university deadline
  • Not currently be studying on another UK-government-funded scholarship

Application timeline

  • 4 August 2026 – Applications open
  • 6 October 2026, 11:00 UTC – Application deadline
  • From October 2026 – Eligibility sifting begins
  • Mid-October 2026 to January 2027 – Reading committee assessments
  • Mid-February 2027 – Interview shortlist announced
  • March–April 2027 – Interviews
  • Mid-June 2027 – Results announced
  • 8 July 2027 – Deadline to submit an unconditional university offer
  • September/October 2027 – Studies begin

How to apply

  1. Create an account on the official Chevening online application system.
  2. Complete the application form, including your personal statement, career plan, and leadership/networking essays.
  3. Submit before the deadline — the form cannot be edited after submission, so draft your essays in advance.
  4. If shortlisted, apply to three eligible UK master's courses and secure an unconditional offer from at least one.
  5. Attend an interview if selected, then await results.

Application tips

  • Start your essays early — strong Chevening essays are specific, not generic, and clearly connect your past experience to your future plans in your home country.
  • Research your three UK course choices properly; admissions officers can tell when a course choice isn't a genuine fit.
  • Have someone review your personal statement for clarity and impact before you submit.

FAQs

Is Chevening only for certain subjects? No, Chevening funds most subjects, though a small number of restricted subjects (such as clinical medicine) are excluded — check the official course search tool.

Can I apply if I'm already studying in the UK? Generally no — Chevening is intended for applicants applying from outside the UK who are not already studying there.

Always verify current dates and eligibility directly on the official Chevening website before applying: chevening.org/scholarships/application-timeline.
