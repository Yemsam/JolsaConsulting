Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you guarantee admission, a scholarship, or a visa?

No. We do not promise jobs, scholarships, admissions, or visas — no consultant honestly can. What we guarantee is a clearer, better-structured, and more competitive application than you started with.

Which countries and application types do you support?

We work with applicants targeting opportunities across Europe, North America, the UK, and beyond, covering job applications, graduate admissions, scholarships, and visa-readiness files for Nigerian, African, and other global clients.

How does the process start?

Most clients start by booking a consultation or taking the free Study-Abroad Readiness Assessment. From there we review your background and goals before recommending the right CV, SOP, scholarship, or visa-readiness support.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Your documents, personal details, and application plans are handled confidentially and are never shared without your permission.

Do you only write documents, or do you also advise on strategy?

Both. We start with strategy — understanding your background, target opportunity, and goals — before writing or reviewing any document, so every CV, SOP, or application file is built around a clear positioning strategy rather than a generic template.
