# Services Pricing Approach

## Goal

Keep the services page clear and easy for clients, while avoiding fixed prices that may not match the real work required.

Some services, such as CV writing, CV review, SOP writing, SOP review, scholarship support, and visa documentation guidance, vary based on the client's profile, document condition, deadline, and level of support needed.

## Recommended Approach

Use clear service cards with either:

- A simple starting price, such as `From ₦XX,XXX`
- Or `Custom quote after assessment` for services that vary more widely

This avoids confusing clients with too many categories while still allowing Jolsa Consulting to price fairly.

## Suggested Page Message

Because every application is different, our fees are based on the type of support, your profile level, document condition, and deadline. Select a service below and complete a short assessment so we can recommend the right option without overcharging or under-supporting you.

## How To Present Each Service

Example:

```text
CV Writing & Review
Best for: students, graduates, professionals, MSc/PhD applicants, and job seekers.
Options: CV review, CV rewrite, or new CV writing.
From ₦XX,XXX
Get quote
```

For wider-scope services, use:

```text
Scholarship Application Package
Eligibility review, essay support, tracker setup, and recommendation guidance.
Custom quote after assessment
Start assessment
```

## Suggested User Flow

1. User sees the service.
2. User clicks `Get Quote` or `Start Assessment`.
3. User answers a short form.
4. Jolsa Consulting reviews the details.
5. User receives the exact fee and timeline.

## Questions For The Assessment Form

- What service do you need?
- What level are you applying at?
- Do you already have a draft?
- What is your deadline?
- Do you want review, rewrite, or full writing support?
- Can you upload the current document, if available?

## Pricing Logic

The final fee can depend on:

- Service type
- New writing vs review
- Student, graduate, professional, MSc, or PhD level
- Document condition
- Number of documents
- Deadline urgency
- Amount of strategy or consultation needed

## Recommendation

Do not show too many pricing categories directly on the services page. Keep the page simple and move the complexity into the quote or booking form.

Use friendly action buttons such as:

- `Get quote`
- `Start assessment`
- `Request pricing`
- `Book consultation`
