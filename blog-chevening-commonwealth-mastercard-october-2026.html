Three Major Scholarships Open Now: Chevening, Commonwealth & Mastercard Foundation (October 2026 Deadlines)

Chevening, Commonwealth Masters, and Mastercard Foundation Scholars applications are open for 2027 entry. Deadlines: September 30–October 20, 2026.

Scholarships · 4 min read

Three major scholarship programmes for study in the UK and South Africa are accepting applications right now, all with October 2026 deadlines. Each is open to Nigerian applicants and covers full funding. Here is what you need to know.

Chevening Scholarships 2027–2028

The UK government's Chevening Scholarship opens applications on 4 August 2026 and closes at 11:00 UTC on 6 October 2026. The award covers university tuition fees, a monthly stipend, and travel costs to and from the UK.

  • Deadline: 6 October 2026 at 11:00 UTC
  • Eligibility: Citizens of Chevening-eligible countries (including Nigeria); undergraduate degree completed at least two years before the application deadline (by October 6, 2024); at least 2,800 hours of work experience acquired after completing the undergraduate degree (full-time, part-time, voluntary work, or internships all count)
  • Funding covers: Full tuition fees, monthly stipend, travel to and from the UK, arrival allowance, homeward departure allowance, cost of one visa application
  • How to apply: Read the application guidance and apply via your country/territory's page on the Chevening website. You must select three different UK master's programmes. Apply at www.chevening.org/scholarships

Commonwealth Master's Scholarships 2027–2028

Applications for Commonwealth Master's scholarships for the 2027/28 academic year will open on Tuesday 8 September and close at 16:00 BST on Tuesday 20 October, for study in the UK beginning in September/October 2027. The Commonwealth Scholarship Commission covers approved airfare and full tuition fees through an agreement with UK universities.

  • Deadline: 16:00 BST on Tuesday 20 October 2026
  • Eligibility: Must be permanently resident in a developing Commonwealth country (Nigeria qualifies); hold a first degree of at least upper second class (2:1) standard by September 2026; unable to afford to study the programme without this scholarship
  • Funding covers: Approved airfare from your home country to the UK and return; full tuition fees covered by agreement between the CSC and the UK university
  • How to apply: Applications are made using the CSC Central application system; the CSC is unable to accept any applications or documentation not submitted via CSC Central. Visit cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk for full details and to apply

Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at University of Cape Town (UCT)

Applications are now open for the 2027 intake of the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at UCT, which provides scholarships and comprehensive support to academically talented young people who face significant social and economic barriers to accessing higher education. Successful applicants must hail from an African country, including South Africa.

  • Deadline: 30 September 2026 for applicants to a UCT bachelor's degree; 31 October 2026 for applicants to an eligible postgraduate diploma in the Faculty of Health Sciences or an honours degree or master's programme at UCT
  • Eligibility: African citizens (including South Africa); students applying for their first bachelor's degree, or for an eligible postgraduate diploma, honours degree or master's degree at UCT; only applicants who receive an offer of admission to UCT will be eligible
  • Funding covers: Comprehensive support for full duration of the chosen programme of study, renewed annually subject to satisfactory academic performance and participation in development programmes
  • How to apply: You must first submit an application for admission to UCT; only applicants who receive an offer of admission to UCT will be eligible for the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program. Full application details at UCT News

All three programmes are highly competitive. Start preparing your documents, references, and personal statements now. Double-check every eligibility requirement and deadline on the official scholarship page before you apply, as details can change.
